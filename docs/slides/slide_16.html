Search plants → Save plant → My Garden → check in + Save observation → compare

  1. Choose a supported plant in a permitted place. Save it and write a question about its flowers or visitors.
  2. Visit on three different dates when possible. Check in after looking; separately save what you actually saw, with the time or weather.
  3. Bring two notes to class. Describe a difference or something that stayed the same, one possible explanation, and one thing you cannot yet conclude.
How does this mission connect to helping biodiversity?

Knowing local plants and their visitors helps you ask better habitat questions. Use a local guide with an adult before proposing diverse native plants or seasonal flowers. Your notes alone do not prove biodiversity increased.
