Saved plant → Add observation → write a note → Save observation

  1. Write one thing you saw and one thing you are unsure about.
  2. Use Save observation for the written record. Make the separate check-in if you checked the plant.
  3. On a later visit, compare your note with what you see now.
What is stronger evidence: “this plant has lots of bees” or a timed note?

A specific note such as “one bee-like visitor during five minutes at noon” is easier to compare and leaves room for uncertainty.
